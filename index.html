Sunprint

A cyanotype of the sunlight in your room. Tell it your city and your window; watch the sun patch cross your floor; click a spot to print a whole year of when it is lit.

The room, from above

Click or drag on the plan to move your spot. With the plan focused, the arrow keys nudge it (Shift for bigger steps). Click or drag on the year print to jump to that moment.

    Everything runs on your computer. Nothing is sent anywhere, nothing is stored. Sun positions use NOAA's low-precision algorithm, accurate to about half a degree; clocks are standard time unless you tick daylight saving. Direct sun only: no clouds, no neighbouring buildings, no trees.
